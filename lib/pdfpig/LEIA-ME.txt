Biblioteca de leitura de PDF (PdfPig) - usada pelo anexo do formulario do GEL
============================================================================

O DICON le o PDF da vistoria do GEL com o PdfPig (licenca Apache-2.0). As DLLs
ja estao COMMITADAS aqui (como o iperf3), entao o anexo do GEL funciona numa
instalacao nova sem passo extra. O Atualizar-DICON.ps1 espelha esta pasta.

Arquivos nesta pasta (nao remover):

  UglyToad.PdfPig.dll                 (PdfPig 0.1.9, lib\netstandard2.0)
  UglyToad.PdfPig.Core.dll
  UglyToad.PdfPig.Fonts.dll
  UglyToad.PdfPig.Tokenization.dll
  UglyToad.PdfPig.Tokens.dll
  System.Memory.dll                   (System.Memory 4.5.5, lib\net461)
  System.Buffers.dll                  (System.Buffers 4.5.1, lib\net461)
  System.Numerics.Vectors.dll         (System.Numerics.Vectors 4.5.0, lib\net46)
  System.Runtime.CompilerServices.Unsafe.dll  (4.5.3, lib\net461)
  Microsoft.Bcl.HashCode.dll          (Microsoft.Bcl.HashCode 1.1.1, lib\net461)

O PowerShell 5.1 (.NET Framework) nao unifica as versoes desses assemblies do
sistema sozinho - o PdfPig pede, p.ex., System.Buffers 4.0.2.0 e a DLL aqui e
outra build. Por isso Read-TextoPdf (src\core\VistoriaGel.ps1) instala um
handler AppDomain.AssemblyResolve que resolve por NOME SIMPLES para a DLL desta
pasta (Register-ResolucaoPdfLib). Nao e preciso app.config nem binding redirect.

Como esta pasta foi montada (uma vez, numa maquina com internet):

  1. Baixe os .nupkg do nuget.org (flat container):
       https://api.nuget.org/v3-flatcontainer/pdfpig/0.1.9/pdfpig.0.1.9.nupkg
       https://api.nuget.org/v3-flatcontainer/system.memory/4.5.5/system.memory.4.5.5.nupkg
       https://api.nuget.org/v3-flatcontainer/system.buffers/4.5.1/system.buffers.4.5.1.nupkg
       https://api.nuget.org/v3-flatcontainer/system.numerics.vectors/4.5.0/system.numerics.vectors.4.5.0.nupkg
       https://api.nuget.org/v3-flatcontainer/system.runtime.compilerservices.unsafe/4.5.3/system.runtime.compilerservices.unsafe.4.5.3.nupkg
       https://api.nuget.org/v3-flatcontainer/microsoft.bcl.hashcode/1.1.1/microsoft.bcl.hashcode.1.1.1.nupkg
  2. Cada .nupkg e um .zip. Extraia as DLLs das pastas lib\ indicadas acima
     (UglyToad.* de lib\netstandard2.0 do pdfpig; as System.*/Microsoft.* das
     pastas net461/net46 dos respectivos pacotes).
  3. Copie so as 10 DLLs listadas para ca (sem .pdb/.xml, sem
     UglyToad.PdfPig.DocumentLayoutAnalysis.dll nem UglyToad.PdfPig.Package.dll).
